TWO FRIDGES — PROGRESSIVE HINTS

Read only one hint at a time.

CONCEPT HINT
There are 201 integer temperatures and only 20,301 sorted pairs. Exhaustive checking is small, robust, and gives the optimal tie choice automatically.

ALGORITHM HINT
Every substance i has an allowed inclusive temperature interval [a_i,b_i]. Choose x<=y in the allowed range [-100,100].
Enumerate x from -100 to 100. For each x, enumerate y from x to 100, guaranteeing increasing lexicographic pair order.

IMPLEMENTATION HINT
For each candidate pair, scan all intervals. The interval is covered when (a_i<=x<=b_i) OR (a_i<=y<=b_i).
If a pair covers ALL intervals, print it immediately: it is the lexicographically smallest valid choice.
After exhausting every candidate, output -1.

CONCEPT QUESTIONS
1. What invariant does your algorithm maintain?
2. Why does it satisfy the requirement rather than only the sample?
3. Which edge case would demonstrate a common error?
