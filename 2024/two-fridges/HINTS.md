# Progressive hints — Two Fridges

<details><summary>Hint 1 · Understand</summary>

What data must be read, and what makes the answer valid? Focus on **Intervals and brute-force search**.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 2 · Reason</summary>

There are only 201 possible integer temperatures. Enumerate the first one from low to high and the second one from the first to 100; verify every interval.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 3 · Implement</summary>

Write pseudocode, prove that the selected strategy works, then test boundary cases. Compare your solution only after attempting it.

</details>

**Concept question:** How does your approach improve on a naive exhaustive strategy? Why is it correct?
