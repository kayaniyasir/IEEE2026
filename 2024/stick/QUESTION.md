# Stick — educational paraphrase of a real 2024 IEEEXtreme problem

[Check original source](https://csacademy.com/ieeextreme-practice/task/stick). This is not a verbatim reproduction of a copyrighted contest statement.

## Problem
N identical squares have side 2L. Each successive square is translated K units right and K units up. Compute the union area of all squares.

## Input
Three integers N K L.

## Output
One integer: the area covered by the squares.

## Constraints and fidelity
Use a wide integer type: the final area may be larger than Java long for extended tests.

## Sample input
```text
3 1 2
```

## Sample output
```text
30
```
