HAPPY NUMBERS — HINTS AND THINKING QUESTIONS

HINT 1 (CONCEPT)
Different large integers can produce the same sum of squared digits. Digit DP aggregates these cases without iterating through the full range.

HINT 2 (APPROACH)
A number is happy when repeated replacement by the sum of squared decimal digits eventually reaches 1; 4 belongs to a known unhappy loop. For a D-digit bound, the first squared-digit sum is at most 81*D, which is much smaller than the number itself.

HINT 3 (IMPLEMENTATION)
Use digit DP for all numbers from 0 to bound X, processing digits left to right and tracking squared-digit sum plus tight/loose relation to X. After reading all digits, add counts whose final sum evolves to 1, excluding sum zero; cache sum classifications. Count the answer in inclusive [L,R] as count(R)-count(L-1), modulo 1000000007 for the repository's illustrative version.
